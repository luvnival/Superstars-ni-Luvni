For Adrianne

Dear Partner In Crime (literally),

Adri, I honestly don’t know how to describe our friendship without mentioning how much we argue over the most random things.

We’re always playfully bickering, annoying each other, and finding something new to fight about, but somehow, you’re still one of the people I feel most comfortable around. I guess that’s just what makes our friendship ours. Thank you for all the random moments, stupid conversations, inside jokes, and memories we’ve made. Thank you for being my friend, my enemy, and somehow my partner in crime all at the same time. Even when we’re constantly annoying each other, I know that I can still count on you when I need someone. You’re one of those people I can be completely myself around without having to think too much about what I say or do. I find comfort in our friendship, even if most of the time that comfort comes with an argument, a joke, or you annoying me for no reason. And honestly, I wouldn’t trade that for anything. I’m really grateful that I got to meet someone like you and have all these random memories with you. We may not always get along, but I hope you know that I genuinely appreciate having you in my life. I’m proud of the person you are, and I’ll always be cheering you on, even if I’ll probably annoy you while doing it.

No matter where life takes us, I hope we never lose the friendship, chaos, and countless arguments that somehow brought us this far. I’ll always treasure the memories we’ve made, and I’m looking forward to all the trouble and memories we still have ahead of us.

— From: Mas bitch pa nimo
