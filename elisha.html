Your generosity and willingness to give to others says so much about the kind of person you are. I hope you always remember to give yourself the same love and patience that you give so freely to everyone else. You deserve to be cared for, understood, and appreciated just as much as you make others feel.

I’m really lucky to have you as my friend, Elisha. Thank you for being someone with such a gentle and loving heart. I’ll always treasure the memories we’ve made, and I hope we continue creating many more together.

— Love, Labni
