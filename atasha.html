No matter where life takes us, I hope you always remember that you have someone who believes in you and will always be cheering you on. I’ll always treasure the memories we’ve made and look forward to all the ones we haven’t made yet.

Thank you for being there for me in both the big moments and the little ones, I’ll always be grateful for you.

— Love, Luv
